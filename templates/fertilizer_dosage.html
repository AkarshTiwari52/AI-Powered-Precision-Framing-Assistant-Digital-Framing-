🚜 Smart Crop Fertilizer & Dosage Guide

Scientifically Correct • Farmer Friendly • High Yield Focused

🌱 Fertilizer / Nutrient 📏 Dosage (Per Hectare) 🕒 When & How to Apply 💡 Why Important (Farmer Notes)
Organic Manure
(FYM / Vermicompost) 		10–20 Tons Basal Application
Buvayi se 15–20 din pehle mitti mein mila dein. 		• Mitti ko zinda banata hai 🌱
• Paani pakadne ki capacity badhata hai
• Chemical fertilizer ka effect improve karta hai
Urea (Nitrogen 46%) 200–250 kg
(3 Split)		 Top Dressing
• 25% Buvayi ke waqt
• 50% Vegetative stage
• 25% Flowering se pehle 		• Patte hara aur growth tez hoti hai
• Neem-coated urea use karein
⚠️ Zyada urea se keet badhte hain
DAP / SSP
(Phosphorus) 		100–150 kg Deep Placement
Beej se 5 cm niche drill method se. 		• Jad (root) majboot hoti hai
• Phosphorus mitti mein move nahi karta
⚠️ Zinc ke saath mix na karein
MOP (Potash 60%) 60–80 kg Split Dose
• 50% Buvayi par
• 50% Dana bharte samay 		• Sukha & disease resistance badhata hai
• Anaaj / phal quality improve hoti hai
Zinc Sulphate 25 kg Soil Application
Har 2–3 saal mein ek baar basal dose ke saath. 		• Chlorophyll banane mein madad
• Paddy mein Khaira rog rokta hai
⚠️ DAP ke saath na milayein
Boron 1–1.5 kg
(500L water)		 Foliar Spray
Pre-flowering stage par. 		• Pollination aur fruit setting better
• Phal girna kam hota hai
NPK 19:19:19
(Water Soluble) 		1–2 kg / 200L Foliar Spray
Subah ya shaam pattiyon par spray. 		• Stress mein fasal ke liye “First Aid” 🚑
• Jaldi recovery hoti hai
Bio-Fertilizer
(Azotobacter / PSB) 		5–10 kg Seed Treatment
Buvayi se pehle beej upchar. 		• Chemical khaad 20% tak kam hota hai
• Mitti ki health improve hoti hai
🌟 Expert Advice:
• Hamesha Soil Test ke baad dosage decide karein
• Drip irrigation ke saath fertigation se 30% khaad bachta hai
• Spray kabhi tez dhoop mein na karein
• 4R Principle follow karein – Right Dose, Right Time, Right Method, Right Place
